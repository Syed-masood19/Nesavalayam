Manifesto

Every thread has a story

We believe every thread has a story and every weaver carries a tradition over generations.

And also we believe clothing should care for the people who wear it and honor the people who create it.

That is why we bring you authentic, skin friendly and affordable handloom clothing that is crafted with care and comfort for everyday wear.

Every purchase that helps weavers earn a more stable income allowing them to continue their craft instead of leaving the profession and also helps to preserve India's rich textile heritage.

Providing fair opportunities by sourcing directly from weavers/weaver groups, more of the value reaches the people who make the products.
